Short User Manual · Home Menu & Services Dashboard

This booklet is for teams running Service Mode — workshops, repairs, sewing, consultancy, and other job-based businesses. It walks through the home screen tiles and every activity inside the Services Dashboard sub-menu.

🏁 First-time setup checklist
  1. Organization: Confirm business name, logo, address, and currency in Settings.
  2. Printer & mPOS: Pair your Bluetooth printer and card reader (if used).
  3. Services Setup: From Services Dashboard → Setup, add job titles, materials, labor rates, and expense categories.
  4. Customers: Add clients as you create jobs, or browse them later under Customers.
  5. First job: Create a New Job, take payment, update status, then print or share the receipt.

1. Home Screen Overview

After login you land on the main tile grid. In Service Mode the tiles are arranged for job work first, then shared business tools.

Tile What it does
SERVICES DASHBOARD Pipeline overview: revenue collected, active/ready counts, recent jobs, and shortcuts to Customers, Analytics, and Setup.
NEW JOB Create a work order / job invoice for a customer (saved offline-first).
ALL JOBS Searchable list of every job with status filters (Pending → Delivered).
CUSTOMERS Customer directory; open a profile to see their job and payment history.
PRINTER & MPOS Connect Bluetooth printer and mPOS card reader. Status dots show device health.
SERVICE RECORDS Historical service / sales style records for completed work and reprints.
PAYMENT HISTORY Audit trail of payments collected across jobs and channels.
CALCULATOR Quick on-device calculator for quotes and change.
SETTINGS Business profile, labels, warranty toggle, currency, sync, and mode options (PIN-protected where configured).
ADMIN HUB Admin tools and elevated controls (access may require verification).
CLOUD METRICS Cloud sync / usage metrics (Pro / Cloud tiers).
FINANCE ANALYTICS Broader finance views for management (secured).
RECONCILIATION Executive reconciliation of collections and settlements (secured).
Top bar at a glance Business name and plan badge (e.g. STANDARD) appear on the left. Date/time sits in the center. Cloud and sync icons on the right show online/offline health; the bell opens notifications; the ⋮ menu opens extra actions.

2. Services Dashboard (Sub-Menu)

Tap SERVICES DASHBOARD to open Services Overview — the daily control room for your job pipeline. Pull down to refresh.

2.1 Profitability summary

The blue banner at the top shows amount collected / total job value. Tap it to jump straight into Services Analytics.

2.2 Snapshot cards

Revenue

Sum of all job totals currently loaded.

Received

Total payments already recorded against those jobs.

Active

Jobs marked IN_PROGRESS.

Ready

Jobs marked READY for pickup/delivery.

2.3 Quick action buttons

👥

Customers

Opens the customer directory from inside the dashboard (same as the home CUSTOMERS tile).

📊

Analytics

Revenue trends, expense breakdown, and date-range reporting for the service business.

⚙️

Setup

Configure job title presets, material categories, parts/prices, labor rates, and expense categories.

2.4 Recent jobs

Shows the latest jobs (up to five). Each row shows title, job ID, remaining balance, and status. Tap a row to open Job Details. Use View All to open the full All Jobs list.

Floating “New Job” button From Services Overview you can also tap the green New Job FAB to create a job without returning to the home grid.

3. New Job

Use NEW JOB (home tile or dashboard FAB) to open a work order. Jobs save offline first (“Save Job Offline”) and sync when the cloud is available.

Create a job — step by step
  1. Customer: Select an existing client or register a new one (name + phone).
  2. Project photo (optional): Take a photo or pick from gallery for “before / expected” reference.
  3. Job type / title: Type freely or pick from presets (e.g. Oil Change, Traditional Dress Sewing).
  4. Description: Notes, measurements, fabric/parts info.
  5. Pricing:
    • Simple: enter one Total Cost.
    • Detailed Billing: toggle on to split Workmanship / Labor and Materials / Parts.
  6. Due date: Optional completion / pickup date.
  7. Warranty: If enabled in Settings, choose duration (1 Month … 3 Years).
  8. Tap Save Job Offline.
Workmanship vs materials Detailed billing keeps professional fees separate from parts. Labor presets and material prices come from Services Setup — configure them once so staff can quote faster.

4. All Jobs & Job Details

4.1 All Jobs list

Search by customer name or job ID. Filter chips: All · Pending · In Progress · Ready · Delivered. Refresh from the app bar if you need a fresh load from storage.

4.2 Job Details actions

Opening a job shows title, status, description, due date, warranty, photo, remaining balance, total cost, and amount paid.

💳

Add Payment

Record a payment against the remaining balance (e.g. cash). The balance and “Received” totals update after confirm.

🛣️

Status

Move the job through Pending → In Progress → Ready → Delivered.

🖨️

Print Receipt

Send the job receipt to the paired Bluetooth printer.

📄

Preview / Share PDF

Generate a professional PDF job invoice to preview, share via WhatsApp/email, or archive.

The Job Log / Bill Breakdown section lists workmanship, materials (qty × price), creation events, and payment notes for an audit trail on the job itself.

5. Customers

The directory lists every registered service customer (name and phone). Tap a customer to open their history ledger — past jobs, balances, and related activity. Customers are usually created during New Job; you can also add them from the customer picker when creating a job.

Client value tip Use a customer’s history to see outstanding balances before starting another job, and to identify your highest-value clients.

6. Services Setup (Dashboard Sub-Menu)

From Services Dashboard → Setup, configure the catalogs staff use every day.

🏷️

Job Titles & Presets

Common job names (Oil Change, Sewing, AC Service, etc.) for fast autocomplete on New Job.

📂

Material Categories

Group parts (Automotive, Building, Fabric, Electronics, etc.).

🧰

Materials & Parts

Define stockable items with default prices for detailed billing.

👷

Labor Settings

Default workmanship / labor fee presets staff can pick while quoting.

🌳

Expense Categories

Cost buckets for service expenses (Fuel, Consumables, Subcontract, etc.) used in analytics logging.

7. Services Analytics

Open from the dashboard quick action or the profitability banner. Default range is the last 30 days; use the date-range icon to change it.

  • Summary cards: Revenue and related performance totals for the selected period.
  • Revenue trend: Chart of collections over time.
  • Expense breakdown: Costs by category.
  • Log Service Expense: Use the chart (+) action to record a service cost against a category.

8. Shared Tools on the Home Grid

8.1 Printer & mPOS

Pair and test your Bluetooth receipt printer and mPOS card reader. The home tile shows small status indicators (e.g. grey / red) so you can see at a glance if a device is offline before taking payment.

8.2 Service Records

Historical record of services rendered — useful for reprints, reviews, and end-of-day checks.

8.3 Payment History

Cross-job payment audit: when money was taken, how much, and against which activity. Use this when reconciling the cash drawer or mPOS settlement.

8.4 Calculator, Settings, Admin Hub

Calculator

Simple math for quotes and change without leaving the app.

Settings

Branding, currency, warranty, labels, sync, and business mode. Some actions need a PIN.

Admin Hub

Elevated administration (users, backups, advanced controls as licensed).

Cloud / Finance / Reconciliation

Pro/Cloud tools for metrics, management finance, and bank/settlement reconciliation.

9. Job Status Lifecycle

Status Meaning Typical next step
PENDING Job booked / quote accepted; work not started. Assign technician; move to In Progress when work begins.
IN_PROGRESS Actively being worked. Update materials/payments as needed; mark Ready when done.
READY Finished and waiting for customer pickup or delivery. Collect remaining balance; print/share receipt; mark Delivered.
DELIVERED Job closed and handed over. Keep PDF/receipt for records; review analytics.

10. Quick Tips

☁️

Offline-first

Jobs and payments save locally. Watch the top-bar cloud icon; when it shows healthy, data will sync.

🔁

Pull to refresh

On Services Overview, pull down after taking payments or changing statuses on another device.

🖨️

Printer first

If Print Receipt fails, open Printer & mPOS and confirm the printer is paired before retrying from Job Details.

✅

Ready = notify customer

When status is Ready, share the PDF or call the customer; collect balance before marking Delivered.

Daily shop routine (suggested)
  1. Check Printer & mPOS status dots on the home tile.
  2. Open Services Dashboard — note Active and Ready counts.
  3. Create New Jobs as customers arrive; take deposits via Add Payment.
  4. Move finished work to Ready; print or share PDFs.
  5. End of day: Payment History + Services Analytics; log any expenses.
