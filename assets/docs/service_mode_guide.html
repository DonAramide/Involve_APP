1. Device Onboarding & Linking

Set up your workshop terminal, bind location telemetry, prevent duplicate email registrations, and synchronize service profiles:

🔧 Step-by-Step Service Onboarding Sequence:
  1. Step 0: Welcome & Existing Profile Linking: Register a new workshop or tap "LINK DEVICE TO EXISTING PROFILE". Tapping the link button opens the camera to scan the QR code from your Web Admin Dashboard (under Fleet > Pair Device), instantaneously downloading all customer records, service catalog items, and active jobs.
  2. Step 1: Technician & Business Profile: Enter Lead Technician First Name, Last Name, Email Address, Web Password (minimum 9 characters including uppercase, lowercase, number, and symbol), Workshop Business Name, Contact Phone Number, and optional Agent Referral Code.
    Live Email Availability Guard: When navigating away from the email field or clicking "NEXT STEP", the app validates whether the email is already registered. If registered, progression to Step 2 is blocked with an alert linking directly to your existing account.
  3. Step 2: Workshop Location & Hardware Telemetry: Enter Workshop Street Address, Country, State, and LGA. The system automatically records GPS coordinates and hardware device identifiers to secure multi-counter job dispatch and offline LAN synchronization.
  4. Step 3: Plan Selection & OTP Verification: Select either a Free 3-Day Trial or enter an official 16-Digit Activation Code. Invify transmits a 6-digit OTP code to your registered Email or WhatsApp. Enter the code to activate your license.

5. New Job Intake & Cost Estimation

📋 How to Book a New Service Job:
  1. Tap New Job on the home dashboard or the + New Job floating button.
  2. Select an existing client from Customers or tap + Add Customer (Name, Phone, Address).
  3. Enter the Job Title (e.g. Maintenance, Brake Pad Replacement, Engine Overhaul).
  4. Enter the detailed Fault Description / Diagnostics note (e.g. Maintenance of washing machine).
  5. Input itemized costs:
    • Workmanship / Labour Fee: Fixed or estimated service charge.
    • Replacement Parts & Materials: Cost of materials from inventory or third-party suppliers.
  6. Set the Expected Delivery Date.
  7. Record initial deposit if paid (Cash, Card, Virtual Account, Transfer).
  8. Tap Create Job & Print Intake Ticket.

8. Payment Channels & Deposit Handling

Service jobs frequently require partial deposits upon intake followed by balance payment at collection. Invify supports all payment channels seamlessly:

Figure 5: Payment Confirmation Screen — Instant confirmation of partial deposit (₦11,750.00 Cash), remaining balance calculation, and print/share options.
Payment Channel Operational Workflow
Cash Payment Tap Add Payment, select Cash, enter amount tendered. Cashier issues printed thermal slip; Remaining Balance updates immediately.
Card (POS / MPOS) Customer taps or inserts card into paired MPOS reader. Card authorization code attaches to the job ledger.
Dynamic Virtual Account (Quasar) Generates an instant 10-digit virtual bank account dedicated to this job ticket. Client transfers from bank app/USSD. Webhook auto-confirms deposit and updates balance.
Direct Bank Transfer Record incoming transfer with bank name and transaction reference code for manual reconciliation.

12. Workshop Standard Operating Procedures

📥

JOB INTAKE & DEPOSIT SOP

  • Book customer and record equipment condition and fault description in New Job.
  • Collect deposit (minimum 50% recommended) and print intake confirmation slip.
  • Assign job to technician; status moves to IN_PROGRESS.
📤

JOB COMPLETION & DELIVERY SOP

  • Technician completes repair and changes status to READY.
  • Customer is notified automatically via WhatsApp.
  • At collection, collect outstanding balance via Cash, Card, or Virtual Account. Status moves to DELIVERED.

13. Troubleshooting & FAQ

Issue Observed Remediation Steps
Customer wants to add more parts mid-repair Open active job in All Jobs, tap Edit Bill Breakdown, add new parts/labour lines, and tap Save & Update Balance.
Client paid deposit via bank transfer but receipt not printed Open All Jobs, select job, tap Add Payment, record bank transfer reference, and tap Print Receipt.
