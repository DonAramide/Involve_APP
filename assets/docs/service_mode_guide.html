Invify Service Mode

User Guide & Operational Manual

Welcome to **Service Mode**. This environment is tailored for service providers, consultants, and professionals. Track your jobs, manage client relationships, and streamline your billing workflow.

🏁 SERVICE SETUP CHECKLIST
  1. Service Catalog: Define your services and hourly rates/fees.
  2. Client Directory: Import or add your client database.
  3. Organization Details: Ensure your professional signature and branding are set.
  4. Work Tracking: Start issuing Job Invoices for completed tasks.

1. Job & Invoice Management

Your hub for billing professional services.

NEW JOB / INVOICE

Generate professional invoices for services rendered.

  • Service Selection: Pick from your predefined catalog.
  • Custom Rates: Adjust fees per job if necessary.
  • Client Assignment: Link every job to a specific client profile.
  • Digital Delivery: Email or WhatsApp invoices directly to clients.

2. Service Catalog

SERVICE DIRECTORY

Manage what you offer and what it costs.

  • Flat Fees vs. Hourly: Flexible pricing structures.
  • Categories: Group services (e.g., Consulting, Maintenance, Delivery).
  • Rate History: Track how your service pricing has evolved.

💡 PRO TIP: Client Retention Use the 'Client Profile' to view the total value of all jobs completed for a specific client. This helps you identify your most valuable professional relationships.

3. Client Administration

CLIENT DIRECTORY

Comprehensive professional relationship management.

  • Activity Ledger: View all invoices and payment history per client.
  • Communication: One-tap access to call or message your clients.
  • Payment Tracking: Monitor outstanding invoices and pending payments.

4. Financial Performance

SERVICE ANALYTICS

Visual reporting for your professional practice.

  • Revenue by Service: See which service generates the most income.
  • Client Acquisition: Track how your client base is growing.
  • Collection Efficiency: Monitor how quickly invoices are being paid.
