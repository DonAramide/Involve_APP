Invify School Mode

Comprehensive User Guide & Feature Manual

Welcome to the Involve App School Mode. This specialized environment is designed specifically for educational institutions to manage fees, students, academic records, and collection analytics with ease.

1. Core Dashboard Functions

NEW TERM BILL

The central point for processing student payments. Search for a student by name or ID, select the applicable fees (Tuition, Books, Uniforms), and generate a professional receipt instantly.

BILLING RECORDS

Access a complete history of every term bill issued. Filter by date, payment status (Paid, Partial, Unpaid), or specific student. Ideal for daily audits and reconciliation.

FEE STRUCTURE / MANAGEMENT

Define your school's financial catalog. Create fee types, set their amounts, and mark essential fees as "Default" to speed up the billing process.

💡 PRO TIP: Carry Forward Logic When you generate a new bill for a student who has an outstanding balance from the previous term, the system automatically adds a "Previous Term Balance" item ensuring no revenue is lost.

2. Academic & Student Administration

STUDENT DIRECTORY

Manage the list of all enrolled students. Store parent contact details, home addresses, and monitor individual financial balances at a glance.

ACADEMIC SETUP

The foundation of your school year. Setup Academic Years (e.g., 2023/2024), define Terms within those years, and manage the list of Classes (JSS 1, Primary 2, etc.).

TEACHER MANAGEMENT

Keep a database of your academic staff, their specialized subjects, and contact information.

3. Advanced Academic Tools

SUBJECT MANAGEMENT

Define the curriculum for your school. Organize subjects by department or level to ensure accurate record-keeping.

RESULT ENTRY

Input student scores for the active term. The system uses your defined "Grading Rules" (found in Academic Setup actions) to calculate performance bands automatically.

STUDENT ANALYTICS

Powerful visual insights. See the percentage of fully-paid vs. owing students, view revenue collection trends by month, and compare expected vs. recovered funds per class.

4. Communication & Utilities

CONTACT DIRECTORY

A unified directory for Management Staff, Academic Staff, and Parents. One-click access to call or message stakeholders directly from the app.
