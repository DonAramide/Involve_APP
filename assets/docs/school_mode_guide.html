1. Device Onboarding & Linking

The onboarding flow activates your school terminal, verifies administrative identity, captures location telemetry, and links hardware to your school cloud profile.

🏫 Step-by-Step School Onboarding Sequence:
  1. Step 0: Welcome & Existing Profile Linking: Register a new institution or tap "LINK DEVICE TO EXISTING PROFILE". Tapping the link button opens the camera to scan the pairing QR code displayed in your Web Admin Dashboard (under Fleet > Pair Device), instantaneously downloading all classes, students, and fee templates.
  2. Step 1: Administrator & School Identity: Enter Administrator First Name, Last Name, School Email Address, Web Password (minimum 9 characters including uppercase, lowercase, number, and symbol), School Name, Contact Phone Number, and optional Agent Code.
    Live Email Availability Guard: When navigating away from the email field or clicking "NEXT STEP", the app validates whether the email already exists. If registered, progression to Step 2 is blocked with an alert linking directly to your existing school profile.
  3. Step 2: Campus Address & Telemetry: Enter Campus Street Address, Country, State, and LGA. The system records GPS coordinates and hardware fingerprinting for offline local network multi-terminal sync across bursary counters.
  4. Step 3: Plan Selection & OTP Verification: Choose between a Free 3-Day Trial or enter an official 16-Digit School Activation Code. Invify transmits a 6-digit OTP code to your registered Email or WhatsApp. Enter the code to activate your license.

7. Fee Payments & Virtual Accounts (Quasar Gateway)

Invify eliminates manual bank teller reconciliation through intelligent automated settlement channels:

⚡

1. DYNAMIC VIRTUAL ACCOUNTS (QUASAR GATEWAY)

When issuing a term bill, Invify generates a dedicated 10-digit account number (e.g. Wema/Providus/Titan) unique to that student's bill. Parents transfer fees via any mobile banking app or USSD. Invify's webhook listener automatically verifies incoming funds within seconds, marks the bill PAID, and sends an automated WhatsApp confirmation to the parent without bursar manual verification.

💳

2. CARD (POS / MPOS) TERMINAL

Parents paying at the bursary counter with debit cards tap or insert their card into the paired MPOS terminal. On PIN authorization, the terminal returns an approval code that prints immediately on the official school fee receipt.

💵

3. CASH COLLECTION

Bursar records exact cash tendered. The system logs cash into the physical bursary float and generates a stamped official receipt.

🏛️

4. DIRECT BANK TRANSFER

For institutional bank deposits. Bursar records the parent's sender bank name, account name, and bank transaction reference code for internal audit matching.

17. Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

🏁

TERM RESUMPTION BURSARY SOP

  • Verify active Academic Session and Term in Academic Setup.
  • Update fee structures for books, tuition, and uniforms in Fees.
  • Generate term bills using New Term Bill. Transmit digital bills with Virtual Account details to parents.
🎓

EXAM & REPORT CARD CLOSING SOP

  • Confirm all CA and exam scores are locked in Result Entry.
  • Verify that students with outstanding fees are flagged before report card release.
  • Batch-export branded PDF report cards for print or WhatsApp dispatch.

18. Troubleshooting & FAQ

Issue Observed Remediation Action
Parent paid via transfer but bill shows unpaid Check if parent transferred to student's unique Virtual Account. If they transferred to the school's general corporate account, open Billing Records, tap Record Payment, select Bank Transfer, and enter the bank reference code manually.
A student changed class or arm mid-session Open Students, select student, tap Edit Profile, update Class/Arm. Historical bills and grades remain preserved.
