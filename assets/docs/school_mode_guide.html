Invify School Mode

Comprehensive User Guide & Feature Manual

Welcome to the Involve App School Mode. This specialized environment is designed specifically for educational institutions to manage fees, students, academic records, and collection analytics with ease.

🏁 GETTING STARTED CHECKLIST
  1. Academic Setup: Define your current Year and active term.
  2. Classes & Subjects: Create your grade levels and curriculum subjects.
  3. Grading Rules: Set up your score ranges (A, B, C, etc.) to enable result entry.
  4. Fee Catalog: Add your tuition and other fee items.
  5. Enrolment: Add students to their respective classes.

1. Core Dashboard Functions

Your daily administrative hub for payments and financial tracking.

NEW TERM BILL (Generate Bill)

The primary tool for issuing receipts.
Sub-Buttons & Actions:

  • Search Bar: Quickly find students by name or admission number.
  • Fee Selection: Tap fees to add them to the bill.
  • Method Dropdown: Choose between Cash, POS, or Transfer.
  • Process Payment: Finalize and print the receipt.

FEE MANAGEMENT

Dashboard for all billing activities and bulk operations.
Sub-Buttons & Actions:

  • (+) Generate Bills Button: Performs batch billing for an entire class at once.
  • Default Toggle: Set fees as "Default" so they are automatically added to every student's bill.
  • Fee History Tab: View all collections sorted by date.

💡 PRO TIP: Carry Forward Logic The system automatically detects outstanding balances from previous terms. When generating a new bill, these balances are added as a "Prev. Balance" item to ensure no revenue is lost.

2. Academic & Student Administration

STUDENT DIRECTORY / PROFILE

Comprehensive student management.
Sub-Buttons & Actions (inside Profile):

  • General Tab: View parent contacts, DOB, and registration data.
  • Billing Records Tab: View all invoices ever issued to this student.
  • Results Tab: Historical view of all academic performance.
  • Payments Tab: Ledger of all cash/electronic payments received.
  • (PAY BALANCE) Button: Quickly process a payment against an existing debt.

ACADEMIC SETUP

Configure the structural settings for your school.
Key Manageable Items:

  • Years/Terms: Set start/end dates and mark the "Active" term.
  • Classes: Add grade levels and assign classroom names.
  • Grading Rules Button: Define the percentage ranges for grades (e.g., 70-100 = A).
  • Subject Management: Manage the curriculum list.

3. Advanced Academic Tools

RESULT ENTRY

Digitize academic performance records.
Sub-Buttons & Actions:

  • Class/Subject Filter: Select the target group for data entry.
  • CA/Exam Fields: Dedicated inputs for Assessment and Examination scores.
  • Dynamic Calculation: Total scores and Grades update instantly as you type.
  • Save Button: Commits all class results to the database at once.

STUDENT ANALYTICS

Visual reporting for management decisions.
Functions & Charts:

  • Payment Status (Pie Chart): Real-time ratio of Paid vs. Owing students.
  • Students per Class (Bar Chart): Identify overcrowded or empty classes.
  • Revenue Trend (Line Graph): Monthly recovery rates for the academic year.

4. Communication & Staff

CONTACT DIRECTORY

Single source of truth for school contacts.
Sub-Buttons & Actions:

  • Management/Academic Tabs: Switch between office staff and teachers.
  • Parents Tab: Searchable list of all student guardians.
  • Action Icons: Direct tap to Call, send SMS, or open a WhatsApp chat.
