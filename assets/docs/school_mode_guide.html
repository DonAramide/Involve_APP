Invify School Mode

Comprehensive User Guide, Dashboard Activities & Sub-Menu Operational Manual

Welcome to Invify School Mode. This handbook provides end-to-end instructions for school proprietors, bursars, administrators, and academic staff. It covers all 20 Dashboard Tiles, dedicated Sub-Menu Activities (Finance, Defaulters, Virtual Accounts, Lesson Notes), and operational workflows for seamless term management.

🏁 Quick-Start Sequence (Beginning of Term)
  1. Academic Setup: Open Academic Setup to define the active Academic Year (e.g. 2026/2027) and select the Current Term.
  2. Classes & Subjects: Configure class arms (e.g. JSS 1 Alpha, Primary 4 Gold) and curriculum subjects.
  3. Fee Catalog & Structure: In Fee Management, attach tuition, boarding, uniforms, and PTA levies to their respective classes.
  4. Enrollment: Add or verify students in the Students directory and ensure they are assigned to current classes.
  5. Bill Generation: Use Fee Management → Batch Billing or New Term Bill to issue bills with automated carry-forward debt.
  6. Hardware Pairing: Check Printer & MPOS to ensure Bluetooth/USB receipt printers are linked and ready.

3. New Term Bill & Billing Records

The school's front-desk billing and collection engine operates with offline-first speed and hardened ledger integrity.

🧾

NEW TERM BILL (Student Point of Sale)

Issue immediate invoices and record fee payments for enrolled students.

  • Student Lookup: Type student name or admission number. The system instantly loads their class and previous debt balance.
  • Item Selection: Select required fee items (Tuition, Books, Uniform, Bus). Quantities and custom discounts can be applied.
  • Carry-Forward Balance: Any unpaid balance from previous terms is automatically appended to the current bill summary.
  • Multi-Channel Payment: Record payments via Cash, Bank Transfer, POS Card Reader, Cheque, or Split Payment.
  • Instant Receipting: Automatically triggers 58mm/80mm thermal receipt printing or generates a detailed A4 PDF receipt.
📊

BILLING RECORDS (Invoice & Receipt Archive)

Complete historical registry of every bill issued by the bursary department.

  • Multi-Filter Search: Filter invoices by Term, Class, Payment Status (Paid, Partial, Unpaid), or Cashier.
  • Reprint & Share: Reprint receipts at any time or share digital PDF receipts directly with parents via WhatsApp or Email.
  • Audited Reversals: Authorize refunds or bill adjustments with compulsory audit remark logging to prevent bursary fraud.
💡 PRO TIP: Idempotent Ledger Protection Invify uses an immutable, idempotent ledger. Even during network drops or power outages, transactions are recorded locally without risk of double-billing or dropped payments.

4. Academic Setup & Subject Registry

Define the institutional structure, grading criteria, and curriculum before starting academic operations.

🏛️ Academic Years & Terms

Create academic sessions (e.g. 2026/2027) and define 3 active terms: First Term, Second Term, and Third Term. Activating a term automatically sets the default billing and grading scope.

🏫 Classes & Arms

Establish grade levels (Creche, Nursery, Primary, JSS, SSS) and create arms (e.g., Primary 1A, Primary 1B). Assign Class Teachers to each arm for automated result compilation.

📐 Grading Rules

Configure grade score bands (e.g., 75-100 = A1 Distinction, 70-74 = B2, 65-69 = B3, etc.), GPA points, and auto-generated teacher remarks for report cards.

📚 Subject Registry

Register curriculum subjects with standard codes (e.g., MTH101, ENG101). Categorize by department (Sciences, Arts, Commercial) and attach to specific classes.

5. Students & Teachers Management

Centralized 360-degree profiles for students, parents, and academic staff.

🎓

STUDENT DIRECTORY & PROFILE HUB

Complete academic and financial lifecycle management for every student.

  • Student Bio & Photo: Full name, admission number, date of birth, gender, house/club, and passport photograph.
  • Parent / Guardian Connect: Store emergency phone numbers and email with 1-tap WhatsApp and direct phone call buttons.
  • Financial Ledger Tab: Real-time statement showing every bill raised, payment made, current balance, and download/print statement button.
  • Academic History Tab: Consolidated report cards across past terms showing GPA trajectory and subject breakdowns.
  • Promotion & Transfer: Single or batch promote students to next higher class arm at the end of the academic year.
👨‍🏫

TEACHER & STAFF DIRECTORY

Manage staff records, assigned classes, and pedagogical workloads.

  • Staff Profiles: Employment IDs, phone contacts, designations, and qualifications.
  • Teaching Allocations: Map teachers to specific class arms and subjects for automatic result entry permissions.
  • Role Assignment: Designate staff roles (e.g., Subject Teacher, Form Master, Bursar, Administrator).

6. Finance Hub & Executive Dashboard

Executive-level financial governance, revenue analytics, and collection oversight. Access is secured by staff PIN authorization.

💼

EXECUTIVE FINANCE DASHBOARD (Gated: Admin / Finance)

High-level command center for the school's financial health.

  • Gross Receivables: Total expected fee revenue for the active term across all enrolled students.
  • Total Collections: Real-time aggregated payments received across all bank accounts and cash registers.
  • Outstanding Debt: Current uncollected balances and defaulter volume.
  • Defaulters Management Sub-Menu: Filter debtors by class or amount, export debtor lists, and send bulk fee reminders.
📈

FINANCE ANALYTICS & REVENUE TRENDS

Deep-dive business intelligence and trend projections.

  • Channel Distribution: Compare revenue share across Bank Transfers, POS, Cash, and Online Virtual Accounts.
  • Collection Velocity: Daily, weekly, and monthly collection curves showing peak payment periods.
  • Term-on-Term Comparative: Benchmark current term performance against previous terms to identify revenue leakage.

7. Virtual Accounts & Automated Reconciliation

Eliminate manual bank teller confirmation with dedicated student virtual account numbers and intelligent reconciliation.

🏦 Dedicated Virtual Accounts

Every student can be assigned a permanent, unique bank account number (e.g. Wema/Moniepoint/Access). When a parent transfers fees to this account, the payment automatically credits the student's ledger instantly.

⚖️ Reconciliation Hub

Review incoming bank payments against invoices. Any unallocated deposits (e.g. transfers with missing student names) appear in the Unmatched Queue for 1-click assignment to the correct student.

🔒 Staff Authorization Required for Financial Modules To protect institutional records, clicking Finance Dashboard, Finance Analytics, or Reconciliation triggers a staff authentication prompt. Only staff with ADMIN, FINANCE, or EXECUTIVE roles can view these sections.

8. Fee Structuring & Fee Catalog

Configure flexible fee policies, compulsory vs optional levies, and itemized school charges.

💳

FEE MANAGEMENT (Class Schedules & Batch Billing)

Define structured fee packages per class arm.

  • Class Fee Rules: Assign specific fee items to classes (e.g. JSS 1 Tuition = ₦120,000; Laboratory Levy = ₦15,000).
  • Compulsory vs Optional: Flag essential fees (Tuition) as compulsory, while making services like School Bus, Lunch, or Clubs optional during billing.
  • 1-Click Batch Bill Generation: Generate invoices for all students in an entire class or the whole school simultaneously with previous debts carried forward.
  • Scholarships & Discounts: Apply fixed or percentage fee waivers to staff children, scholarship recipients, or siblings.
📦

FEES & PRODUCTS CATALOG (Stock / Items)

Inventory and price management for chargeable school items.

  • Item Catalog: Add Tuition, Uniforms, Exercise Books, Cardigans, Badges, ID Cards, and Excursions.
  • Inventory Tracking: Track physical stock quantities for uniforms and books with automated reorder alerts.
  • Category Tagging: Categorize items under Tuition, Academic Supplies, Uniforms, or Sundry Levies.

9. Result Entry & Digital Grading

Fast, error-free academic assessment compilation with automated grade, position, and remark calculation.

📝 Step-by-Step Result Recording Procedure
  1. Tap RESULT ENTRY on the dashboard.
  2. Select the Academic Year, Term, Class Arm, and Subject.
  3. Enter scores in the spreadsheet-style grid for CA 1 (e.g. 20), CA 2 (e.g. 20), and Terminal Exam (e.g. 60).
  4. The system automatically computes the Total (100), assigns the Grade Letter (A, B, C, D, F) based on grading rules, and updates class rankings.
  5. Tap Save / Submit. Class masters can preview and print batch report sheets directly.

Standardize pedagogical preparation with rich lesson planning tools and AI-assisted generation.

📝

LESSON NOTES HUB & AI WIZARD

Empower teachers to draft, organize, and submit structured lesson plans.

11. Printer, MPOS & Connectivity

Configure peripheral hardware and POS terminals for seamless payment collection and receipting.

🖨️ Printer Pairing & Setup

Connect thermal printers via Bluetooth, USB, or LAN (Network). Supports 58mm and 80mm ESC/POS printers, built-in Sunmi POS printers, and desktop A4 printers. Use Test Print to verify alignment.

💳 MPOS Card Terminal Pairing

Pair Bluetooth card readers for debit card fee payments. The tile shows dual status indicators: Green dot for connected printer and Blue dot for configured POS terminal.

13. Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)

Recommended step-by-step routines for daily bursary operations and term milestones.

☀️ Daily Bursary Collection Flow
  1. Power on terminal and verify printer status (Green dot on tile).
  2. Open New Term Bill, look up student, select paid fee items.
  3. Record payment channel (Cash, Transfer, POS) and print customer receipt.
  4. At end of day, open Transaction History to verify register totals against physical cash in drawer.
🏁 End-of-Term Processing Routine
  1. Ensure all subject teachers complete Result Entry.
  2. Generate and inspect draft report sheets.
  3. Open Finance Dashboard → Defaulters to audit remaining debts.
  4. In Academic Setup, create the upcoming term and roll forward balances.

14. Troubleshooting & Frequently Asked Questions

Issue Observed Probable Cause Recommended Resolution
Printer status dot is Red Bluetooth disconnected or printer powered off. Turn on printer, ensure paper roll is loaded, go to Printer & MPOS and tap Connect.
Student has unrecorded debt Previous term bill was not generated or unpaid. Open student profile → Ledger Tab to inspect statement or manually adjust balance with an audited bill.
Bank transfer not reflecting on student Payment sent without reference or virtual account. Open Reconciliation Hub → Find the transaction in Unmatched Payments and click Assign to Student.
Cannot access Finance / Admin Insufficient staff role or incorrect PIN. Verify staff credentials. Admin/Finance privileges must be configured in Teachers & Staff settings.
