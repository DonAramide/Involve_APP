Strategic Value of the Decoupled Tablet Architecture
Why separating the workflow screen from the cryptographic PIN pad wins

1. Reduced PCI Scope

The tablet never touches plaintext card PIN data. All cryptographic operations remain quarantined inside the AISINO VM30.

2. Lower Maintenance Cost

Screen damage or tablet replacement does not require expensive re-keying or cryptographic recertification of the payment device.

3. Rich UI Ergonomics

Operators manage complex class rosters, term bills, and inventories on a comfortable screen instead of a cramped 2-inch keypad.
