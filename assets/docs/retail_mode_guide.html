Invify Retail Mode

User Guide & Operational Manual

Welcome to **Retail Mode**. This environment is optimized for physical stores and merchandise-based businesses. Manage inventory, track sales, and grow your customer base with precision.

🏁 RETAIL SETUP CHECKLIST
  1. Product Catalog: Add your products with selling and cost prices.
  2. Inventory Inflow: Record your current stock levels.
  3. Customer Directory: Build your database of frequent shoppers.
  4. Sales Terminal: Start recording daily transactions.

1. Sales & Transactions

Your point-of-sale hub for daily revenue.

NEW INVOICE / SALE

Generate receipts for customers.

  • Product Search: Quickly find items by name or category.
  • Multi-Item Billing: Add multiple products to a single sale.
  • Flexible Payment: Choose Cash, POS, or Bank Transfer.
  • Instant Receipt: Print or share the digital invoice.

2. Inventory & Stock

STOCK MANAGEMENT

Maintain precise control over your merchandise.

  • Inventory Tracking: Automatic deduction of stock upon sale.
  • Low Stock Alerts: Get notified when items need replenishment.
  • Stock History: Audit trail of additions and adjustments.
  • Categories: Organize products for better reporting.

💡 PRO TIP: Cost vs. Profit Always enter the 'Cost Price' for your products. The system will automatically calculate your real-time gross profit in the Analytics dashboard.

3. Customer Relations

CUSTOMER DIRECTORY

Keep track of your loyal buyers.

  • Purchase History: View what each customer has bought over time.
  • Contact Management: Easy access to phone numbers for marketing.
  • Outstanding Balances: Track customers who owe for credit sales.

4. Business Insights

RETAIL ANALYTICS

Data-driven decisions for your store.

  • Top Selling Items: Identify your most popular products.
  • Revenue Trends: Track daily, weekly, and monthly growth.
  • Profitability Reports: See your actual margins after costs.
