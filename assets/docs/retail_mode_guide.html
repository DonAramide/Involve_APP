1. Initial Setup & Device Onboarding

Invify features an enterprise onboarding wizard designed to verify organizational identity, prevent duplicate accounts, bind device telemetry, and ensure rapid POS deployment.

🚀 Step-by-Step Onboarding Sequence:
  1. Step 0: Welcome & Existing Profile Linking: Choose between registering a new business or tapping "LINK DEVICE TO EXISTING PROFILE". Tapping the link button opens the camera to scan the QR code from your Web Admin Dashboard (under Fleet > Pair Device), immediately syncing all store data without re-entering credentials.
  2. Step 1: Identity & Mode Setup: Enter First Name, Last Name, Email Address, Web Password (minimum 9 characters including uppercase, lowercase, number, and symbol), Business Name, Phone Number, and optional Agent Code.
    Live Email Availability Guard: The email field validates in real-time. When leaving the email field or tapping "NEXT STEP", the app queries the verification engine. If the email is already registered, navigation is blocked, and an alert provides direct shortcuts to link the existing account or enter a different email.
  3. Step 2: Address & Device Telemetry: Enter your Street Address, Country, State, and LGA. The system automatically captures GPS coordinates and hardware device identifiers to secure offline peer sync.
  4. Step 3: Plan Selection & OTP Verification: Select either a Free 3-Day Trial or enter an official 16-Digit Activation Code. Invify transmits a 6-digit OTP code to your registered Email or WhatsApp. Enter the code to activate your license.

4. All Payment Methods & Virtual Accounts

Invify provides full multi-tender checkout support to handle all Nigerian retail payment preferences:

💵

1. CASH TENDER

Cashier enters amount tendered. The system calculates change, pops the connected cash drawer, logs cash-on-hand into the Internal Ledger, and prints a receipt.

💳

2. CARD (POS / MPOS)

Select Card payment. Invify transmits the exact amount to the paired Bluetooth/USB MPOS terminal. Customer inserts chip card and inputs their 4-digit PIN. On approval, the authorization code is recorded directly on the digital invoice.

⚡

3. DYNAMIC VIRTUAL ACCOUNTS (QUASAR GATEWAY)

Select Virtual Account. The system instantly generates a dedicated, dynamic 10-digit account number (e.g. Wema/Providus/Titan) displaying the store name and exact invoice amount. The customer transfers funds via their mobile banking app or USSD. Invify's webhook listener automatically verifies incoming funds within seconds and marks the invoice PAID without manual cashier confirmation.

🏛️

4. DIRECT BANK TRANSFER

For standard institutional bank transfers. Cashier selects the bank, enters the payer's bank name, sender account name, and bank transaction reference for manual reconciliation.

5. New Invoice & Point of Sale (POS)

🛒 Standard POS Checkout Procedure:
  1. Tap New Invoice on the home dashboard.
  2. Add items using any of three methods:
    • Barcode Scanner: Scan product barcode via wireless hand scanner or device camera.
    • Item Grid / Categories: Tap category pills (e.g. Drinks, Provisions) and tap product cards.
    • Search Bar: Type product name or SKU for instant autocomplete.
  3. Adjust quantities with + / - buttons. Apply line discounts if authorized.
  4. Select customer (optional for walk-ins, required for credit/debt sales).
  5. Tap Proceed to Checkout, choose Payment Method (Cash, Card, Virtual Account, Transfer), finalize payment, and issue customer receipt.

6. Stock Management & Category Sub-Menu

Accurate inventory control prevents stock-outs, identifies shrink, and computes automated gross profit margins:

Activity Action & Parameters Result & Audit Trail
Add Product Name, Category, Cost Price, Selling Price, Initial Stock, Barcode, Low-Stock Alert Level. Creates master inventory record with profit margin calculation.
Stock In / Restock Select item, enter incoming shipment quantity, supplier name, and invoice cost. Increments stock count, computes weighted average cost, logs purchase entry.
Stock Adjustment Select item, record damaged, expired, or shrinkage units with reason note. Decrements stock without recording sales revenue; logged in damage report.
Manage Categories Create, rename, or color-code product groups (e.g. Toiletries, Beverages). Updates POS quick-filter buttons and sales breakdown reporting.

7. Inventory Valuation & Stock Reports

Tap Inventory Report to generate instant financial stock valuation metrics:

  • Total Stock Items: Cumulative count of unique active SKUs.
  • Total Inventory Cost Value: Sum of (Stock On Hand × Cost Price). Represents tied-up capital.
  • Total Retail Market Value: Sum of (Stock On Hand × Selling Price). Expected gross realization.
  • Projected Gross Profit: Difference between Market Value and Cost Value.
  • Reorder Alerts: Highlighted list of items currently at or below their designated reorder threshold.

8. Customer Lookup & Debt Ledgers

Track frequent buyers, corporate accounts, and manage credit sales without paper books:

  • Credit Sales: Complete checkout under "On Account / Credit". The balance is automatically added to the customer's debt ledger.
  • Debt Recovery / Repayments: Open Customer Lookup, search customer, tap "Record Payment", input amount and payment channel. The customer's debt balance updates instantly and a payment receipt is issued.
  • Customer Statements: Generate WhatsApp or PDF account statements showing all historical purchases and repayments.

12. Hardware: Printers & MPOS

Invify interfaces natively with industry-standard POS hardware:

🖨️ Thermal Receipt Printer Pairing:
  1. Power on your 58mm or 80mm Bluetooth / USB thermal receipt printer.
  2. On the dashboard, tap Printer & MPOS.
  3. Tap Scan Devices. Select your printer model from the list.
  4. Tap Pair & Connect. Enter PIN 0000 or 1234 if prompted.
  5. Tap Test Print. The printer will print a test diagnostics slip verifying connection.

13. Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

🌅

MORNING STORE OPENING SOP

  • Power on POS tablet/terminal and thermal printer.
  • Verify online or LAN sync indicator in the top bar.
  • Open Printer & MPOS, perform test print.
  • Verify opening cash drawer float in Finance Analytics.
🌙

NIGHT STORE CLOSING & SHIFT AUDIT

  • Verify Cloud Metrics to ensure Offline Operations Queue is at 0.
  • Open Invoice History, select the day's date, record Total Cash, Card, and Virtual Account sales.
  • Count physical cash in register drawer; match against Cash on Hand.
  • Tap Admin Hub > Close Register Shift to lock the audit ledger and print the daily Z-Report.

14. Troubleshooting & Frequently Asked Questions

Issue Observed Immediate Remediation Steps
Printer does not print receipts Check paper roll direction. Open Printer & MPOS, verify device shows connected. If disconnected, unpair and tap scan to re-pair.
Internet goes down mid-transaction No action required. Invify operates 100% offline. Take cash or card payments as usual; sales queue automatically in Cloud Metrics and sync when reconnected.
Customer claims bank transfer sent but till didn't verify Open Invoice History, tap Pending Transfers. Verify bank reference number. Once confirmed on bank app, supervisor can manually mark invoice as paid.
