Invify Retail Mode

Complete User Manual, Dashboard Activities & Sub-Menu Operational Field Guide

Welcome to Invify Retail Mode. Engineered for supermarkets, boutiques, electronics shops, pharmacies, wholesalers, and general merchandise retail stores. This manual covers all 13 Dashboard Tiles, dedicated Sub-Menu Activities (Stock Management, Valuation Reports, Customer Debt Ledgers, Financial Analytics), and high-speed point-of-sale workflows.

🏁 Quick-Start Sequence (Store Setup)
  1. Store Profile: In Settings, set your Business Name, Store Address, Phone Numbers, Currency Symbol (₦, $, £, €), and upload your Logo.
  2. Categories: Open Stock → Manage Categories to organize items (e.g. Groceries, Drinks, Toiletries, Electronics).
  3. Product Catalog: Add products with Cost Price, Selling Price, Initial Stock Quantity, and Barcodes.
  4. Hardware Pairing: Go to Printer & MPOS to link your 58mm/80mm Bluetooth/USB receipt printer and card reader.
  5. Customers: Add frequent shoppers or wholesale clients in Customer Lookup to track credit sales and loyalty.
  6. First Sale: Open New Invoice, scan or select products, take payment, and issue a thermal receipt.

3. New Invoice & Point of Sale (POS)

The core billing engine designed for high-volume counter sales with offline-first reliability.

🛒

NEW INVOICE (Checkout Counter)

Rapid item scanning, cart adjustments, and flexible multi-channel payment collection.

  • Barcode Scanning & Quick Search: Use USB/Bluetooth laser scanners, device camera, or text search to add items in milliseconds.
  • Cart Adjustments: Adjust quantities, apply item-level or invoice-level discounts, and toggle tax/VAT.
  • Customer Assignment: Attach sales to registered customers to track purchases or record credit/debt sales.
  • Multi-Channel Payment: Accept Cash, POS Card, Bank Transfer, Store Credit, or Split Payment (e.g. 50% Cash + 50% Transfer).
  • Instant Receipt Printing: Auto-triggers 58mm/80mm thermal slips or generates digital A4 PDF receipts.
⚡ 4-Step Cashier Checkout Procedure
  1. Tap NEW INVOICE from the dashboard or press shortcut key.
  2. Scan products or search and tap items to add to the cart.
  3. Tap Charge / Pay, select the payment method, and enter amount tendered.
  4. Collect payment, hand customer the printed receipt and change. The stock is automatically deducted in real time.

4. Stock Management & Category Sub-Menu

Complete inventory lifecycle management with cost tracking, profit margin calculation, and category hierarchies.

📦 Product Registration & Editing

Enter Product Name, Barcode/SKU, Category, Cost Price, and Selling Price. The system automatically computes your gross profit margin percentage per unit.

🏷️ Manage Categories Sub-Menu

Access Stock → Manage Categories to organize merchandise into logical departments (e.g. Beverages, Provisions, Toiletries, Electronics, Fashion).

📥 Stock In / Restocking

Record new supplier deliveries. Add incoming quantities to existing stock with updated cost prices to maintain accurate inventory valuation.

⚠️ Low Stock & Reorder Alerts

Set reorder threshold levels per item. Products running low are highlighted in orange/red to prevent stockouts.

💡 PRO TIP: Accurate Cost Prices = Accurate Profits Always record the Cost Price (Purchase Price) for every item. This enables the system to calculate your exact net margins and daily store profitability in Finance Analytics.

5. Inventory Valuation & Stock Reports

Real-time financial visibility into total merchandise investment and projected revenues.

📊

INVENTORY REPORT & VALUATION

Executive inventory auditing and financial metrics.

  • Total Stock Cost: Total capital currently tied up in physical inventory at purchase cost.
  • Total Retail Value: Gross expected revenue when all current merchandise is sold at retail prices.
  • Projected Gross Profit: Projected total profit margin across all warehouse and store stock.

7. Sales Records & Transaction History

Complete audit trails for all sales, register receipts, payment methods, and cash balancing.

🧾 Sales Records (Invoices)

Search and filter all past sales by Date, Customer, Payment Status (Paid, Partial, Unpaid), or Cashier. Reprint customer receipts or send PDF invoices.

🔍 Transaction History (Audit Trail)

Immutable chronological log of every monetary event. Displays payment channels (Cash, POS, Transfer), cashier shift totals, and cash-in-drawer reconciliation.

⚠️ Refund & Reversal Governance Sales refunds or item returns require manager authorization and a documented reason. All reversals are permanently logged in the audit trail to eliminate cashier shrinkage.

Executive financial business intelligence and automated bank settlement matching. Access is secured by staff PIN authorization.

FINANCE ANALYTICS (Gated: Admin / Finance)

Visual business intelligence and store growth analytics.

Verify that money received in your bank matches sales recorded on the till.

🔒 Staff Authorization Required for Financial Sections Accessing Finance Analytics or Reconciliation triggers a secure staff authentication prompt. Only accounts with ADMIN, FINANCE, or EXECUTIVE privileges can view financial reports.

11. Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)

Standard routines for cashiers and store managers to ensure 100% financial and inventory accuracy.

☀️ Morning Shift Opening Checklist
  1. Power on terminal and verify Bluetooth printer status (Green dot).
  2. Count opening cash float in the register.
  3. Ensure barcode scanner is plugged in and responding.
  4. Verify that the Live Clock and Sync Status icons are active.
🌙 Evening Shift Close & Balancing Flow
  1. Open Transaction History to view the daily sales summary.
  2. Count physical cash in the drawer and match against the Cash Total.
  3. Verify POS card terminal settlement slips against the POS Total.
  4. Open Reconciliation to confirm all bank transfers.
  5. Perform an offline database backup in Settings.

12. Troubleshooting & Frequently Asked Questions

Issue Observed Probable Cause Recommended Action
Printer dot is Red / Not printing Bluetooth disconnected, out of paper, or cover open. Check paper roll, power cycle printer, open Printer & MPOS, and tap Connect. Run a Test Print.
Barcode scanner not finding item Barcode not registered or scanned with extra prefix. Open Stock, edit the product, tap the barcode field, and rescan the product barcode to map it correctly.
Negative or wrong stock count Items sold before entering supplier restock invoice. Open Stock, search the item, and tap Restock / Stock In to update quantity to actual physical count.
Cloud icon shows Red (Offline) Internet connection down or online sync disabled. Sales continue uninterrupted offline. When internet is restored, the system automatically syncs pending invoices to the cloud.
Access Denied to Finance Analytics Staff account lacks Manager/Admin privileges. Authenticate using an Admin or Finance staff PIN to unlock financial dashboards.
